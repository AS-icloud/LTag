6 · QR Scan-Reliability Settings ▾

Every QR window reserves this many blank modules of solid white on all sides before any data modules are drawn, and the live capacity check (step 2) refuses to report a fit smaller than this module size — both defaults follow common print-scan-reliability guidance.

"Real / actual size" builds a custom page sized exactly to your design plus these margins — no wasted paper, and no fixed A6/A5/A4 choice to worry about (handy for label printers or die-cut sheets). "Virtual paper" places the design on a standard page size instead, useful for printing on ordinary printer paper and cutting it out.
